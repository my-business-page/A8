WHY SHIZUKU

A tiny studio
for serious tea.

S

Shizuku Lab is a home-based specialty matcha studio in Toa Payoh, Singapore, built around freshly whisked Japanese tea and small weekend preorder collections.

The focus is simple: first-harvest matcha when you want green, lively depth; dark-roasted houjicha when you want something toasted and calm.

Drinks are prepared to order rather than sitting finished and waiting. Check the current drop, choose your drink, collect during the announced window, and take the quiet cup with you.
