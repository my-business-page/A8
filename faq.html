GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Is Shizuku Lab a walk-in café?

No. It is a home-based studio operating through preorder and self-collection.

When can I collect?

The studio operates on weekend preorder collections. Current hours are directed to Instagram and the live order flow.

What milk is used?

Oat milk is the default for latte drinks.

Matcha or houjicha?

The studio specialises in first-harvest Japanese matcha and dark-roasted houjicha.

Can I visit without an order?

Please don't. Collection should be tied to a confirmed preorder and its stated window.

What about allergens?

Ingredients vary by drink and seasonal release. Review the current order information and contact the studio through its active channel if you have an allergy.
